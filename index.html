A TOWER DEFENSE TOOLKIT

Interactive tool to learn Tower Defense design Final Degree Project (TFG) Solo project

A Tower Defense Toolkit is a playable tool that teaches how Tower Defense levels are designed. Instead of choosing premade towers with fixed stats, you build the tower yourself: damage, attack speed, range, area shape, damage type and more, all inside a limited point budget. Every level is a parametric design challenge that needs a specific configuration to be beaten.

A level full of fast, weak enemies asks for a very different tower than a level with a few tough ones. The point budget makes sure no configuration is the best everywhere, so the real challenge is to understand why each parameter works in each situation. You learn how the genre is designed by playing with its numbers.

I chose Tower Defense because it is a parametric genre by nature: it depends on connected numerical variables, it gives immediate feedback, and it needs no previous skill, so all the attention goes to design decisions instead of execution.

Key Features

  • 6 levels: a tutorial and 5 design labs, about 50 minutes to 1 hour without retries
  • 14 tower parameters, from damage, attack speed and range to critical hits, slow, stun, burn, poison, bounce and 6 area shapes
  • Point budget and exclusive parameters, so there is never a universally optimal tower
  • Enemy stats such as health, speed, armor, regeneration and resistances, with enemy roles you can read at a glance
  • Floating damage numbers and health bars that make the hidden variables visible
  • Feedback screen after each level that shows how well the knowledge was applied
  • Labs can be played in any order, or in the recommended order for guided learning

The Labs

  • Tutorial (Level 0): nine phases that unlock one system at a time: placing towers, launching waves, upgrading parameters, earning gold and the win and lose conditions
  • Camera Lab: defend the same level from an isometric view and then from a top-down view, to see how perspective changes reading of space and decisions
  • Parameter Unlock Lab: the 14 parameters are introduced one by one, each with a wave designed to make it essential, and a final challenge with every enemy type
  • DPS Check Lab: free experimentation with no economy limit and no defeat. Reach and hold a damage per second threshold for five seconds and get an efficiency score
  • Synergies Lab: only 3 active parameters per tower, so you combine specialised towers instead of building one that does everything
  • Wave Design Lab: you design the attacking wave with a threat budget, watch it hit the base, and then have to defend against the wave you created

Design Process

  • Analysed Plants vs. Zombies, Kingdom Rush and Bloons TD 6 to define tower placement (anchor points on the map, as in Kingdom Rush) and the core variables of the genre
  • Used Platformer Toolkit by Mark Brown as the main reference for how to teach design through exposed parameters
  • Structured the analysis and the design with the DDE framework (Design, Dynamics, Experience)
  • Cut parameters and enemy stats such as incremental attack speed, accumulator, split, shield and explosion because they added play value but little teaching value
  • Pivoted in the fourth iteration after a review with my tutor: the game was too close to a regular Tower Defense, so I changed from tutorialised levels to specialised labs, each one teaching a single design principle

Playtesting and Balance

  • Internal playtests during development, plus informal feedback from people outside the project and my tutor
  • Re-tuned parameter costs and the difficulty curve of the waves, and increased gold income in phases that were too restrictive
  • Added checkpoints between waves so players can experiment without losing too much progress
  • Gave each enemy role (tank, fast, invisible, rage, regenerator) its own texture identity so the role is clear immediately

HELLION

Fast paced roguelike boomer shooter 4 Months Development Team of 7

Hellion is a fast-paced roguelike boomer shooter built around high-mobility FPS combat and retro-inspired action. Every run takes place in a different procedurally generated level, so the player has to keep moving, read the arena and adapt. The goal was a game that feels challenging but fair and makes you want to start one more run.

Key Features

  • Arena based fast paced shooter with high-mobility combat
  • Roguelike elements: every run is different, with procedural levels drawn from 20+ room designs
  • 10 different power-ups
  • 5 enemy types and a final boss encounter
  • A variety of weapons
  • Challenging but fair

My Contribution

  • Designed the 20+ rooms, the 10 power-ups, the 5 enemy types and the final boss encounter
  • Built the core gameplay loops and the level design that make a run replayable
  • Worked in a team of 7

SODAINA SENSHI

Hotline Miami meets Katana Zero 3 Months Development Team of 6

Sodaina Senshi is a cinematic beat 'em up. You only have your katana to fight the Onis and save the temple. The design goal was skill-based swordplay: fast, readable and satisfying to master, wrapped in a strong cinematic presentation.

Key Features

  • Satisfying katana combat. Slash 'em!
  • Parry and deflect
  • 4 enemy types and 20+ rooms to explore
  • Multiple phases final boss battle with 3 health bars
  • Highly detailed pixel art by Jan Puig

My Contribution

  • Designed the combat mechanics and the cinematic presentation
  • Designed the final boss: 3 health bars and several different phases
  • Worked in a team of 6
